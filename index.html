Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Cinema Makeup different from regular beauty makeup?
Cinema Makeup focuses on creating characters, emotions, and illusions. You’ll go beyond glam to explore SFX, prosthetics, aging effects, blood work, and fantasy looks—skills that are essential in film, theatre, cosplay, and entertainment.
Will I get to build a portfolio during the course?
Yes. You'll have the opportunity to document your practical work and create a basic portfolio by the end of the course. We also offer additional portfolio-building support for students who want to pursue advanced work or industry placements.
How is this course taught—online or in person?
All Cinema Makeup courses are taught in person at our Dubai campus. This hands-on learning approach is essential when working with prosthetics, molds, and materials that require in-studio practice.
What materials or makeup kits will I need?
We provide a specially curated Student Makeup Kit as part of your course fee. It includes the essentials needed for all demonstrations and practice sessions during the course.
How do I know if this is the right course for me?
If you’re curious about character transformation, love fantasy or horror films, or dream of working behind the scenes in cinema, this course is your perfect starting point. Book a free counselling session to explore whether it aligns with your goals.
What kind of career paths can I explore after completing this course?
Graduates go on to assist on film sets, work backstage in theatre, join production teams, or freelance in areas like SFX makeup, cosplay, content creation, and more. This course opens multiple doors across creative industries.
Will I receive one-on-one feedback on my work?
Absolutely. With small batch sizes (maximum 6 students), our instructors provide personalized feedback and mentorship throughout the course, helping you improve your skills with confidence.
Do you offer support after the course ends?
Yes. Our commitment doesn’t end at certification. You'll gain access to our alumni network, continued career guidance, invites to exclusive events, and product discounts designed to support your ongoing growth.
